A specific environmental question.

Environmental change can be studied at a regional scale, but it can also be approached through a particular material and a local water system. This project focused on the environmental impact of tire rubber particles in the rivers and lakes of Jinan.

The project offered another context for exploring water-related environmental questions, alongside my interest in broader patterns in monitoring data.

My participation.

From January 8 to February 8, 2026, I participated in the research project and independently completed a research report as part of the work.

PROJECT TITLE

Environmental impact of tire rubber particles on the water bodies of rivers and lakes in Jinan

Keeping the account precise.

A Certificate of Practice dated February 10, 2026 documents the participation period and completion of the report. This short note describes that documented experience without extending it into claims about independent experimental design or specific findings.

The report, experimental data, and original certificate are not distributed through this website. No sampling sites, concentrations, treatment efficiencies, or research conclusions are inferred from the project title.
